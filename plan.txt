Godot 4.6 Steal Poker 2d game

players:
4 ( 3 CPU players and 1 human)

Use assets from the folder.

rules:
-cpu deals 5 cards to each player
-5% ante
-flop one card on the table
-pot limit (10%) betting round
-everyone select 0 to 5 cards to discard and re draw
-pot limit (10%) betting round
-everyone shows their hands and steal one card from the player to the left on countdown timer from 15 seconds
-flop one card on the table
-judge player hands according to poker rules
